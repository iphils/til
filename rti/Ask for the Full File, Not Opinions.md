# Ask for the Full File, Not Opinions

When filing an RTI, do not ask for the opinion or advice of the Public Information Officer (PIO).
PIOs are not legally bound to provide opinions or interpretations — such requests are routinely rejected.

Instead, ask for the **complete file** related to the matter, specifically including:

- File notings by all concerned officials
- Internal correspondence (between departments, officials, etc.)
- External correspondence (letters, emails, communications with outside parties)

## Why this works

The notings recorded by different officials as a file moves through the system reveal:

- **The why** — reasoning and justifications behind decisions
- **The how** — the process, approvals, and sequence of events

This gives you a far more complete and factual picture of the matter than any summary or opinion ever would.
