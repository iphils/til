# Ask for Appellate Authority Email and Contact

PIOs often omit the email address and contact number of the Appellate Authority in their reply — this appears to be a deliberate omission that forces you to send a physical appeal by post, which costs money and takes time.

To avoid this, **always include the following as one of your RTI questions:**

> Please provide the name, designation, email address, and contact number of the First Appellate Authority to whom an appeal can be filed under Section 19(1) of the RTI Act, 2005, in case the applicant is not satisfied with this reply.

## Why this matters

- Filing an appeal by email is free and instant.
- Filing by post costs money (stamp paper, postage) and takes days.
- Without the email, you may not know which office or officer to address the appeal to.
- The PIO is obligated to provide this information — asking for it explicitly puts it on record.

## What to do if still not provided

If the PIO still does not provide this information in the reply, note that:

- The name and contact of the Appellate Authority is often listed on the public authority's website under the RTI section.
- You can also refer to the [RTI Portal](https://rtionline.gov.in) if the public authority is registered there.
- The omission itself can be grounds for a complaint to the Central/State Information Commission.
