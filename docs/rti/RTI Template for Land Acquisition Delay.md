# RTI Template: Why Is the Land Acquisition for My Project Delayed?

If a road widening, infrastructure, or development project near you seems stalled, you can use RTI to find out exactly where the land acquisition process stands.

**How to use this template:** Replace the highlighted placeholders (project name, district, year range) with details relevant to your project. File with the District Collector's office of the concerned district, or the PWD/KRFB office depending on who is executing the project.

---

## RTI Application Template

**Subject:** Information regarding land acquisition for [PROJECT NAME], [DISTRICT] District

Respected Sir/Madam,

I am seeking specific information under the Right to Information Act, 2005 regarding the land acquisition process for the **[PROJECT NAME]** in **[DISTRICT]** District.

I request certified copies of the following specific documents:

1. Copy of the Preliminary Notification issued under **Section 11(1) of the RFCTLARR Act, 2013** for the [PROJECT NAME]. If not issued, please state the same in writing with reasons.

2. Copy of the Declaration/Notification issued under **Section 19(1) of the RFCTLARR Act, 2013** for this project. If not issued, please state the same in writing.

3. Copy of the Government Order granting **administrative sanction** for this project.

4. Complete list of **file numbers** pertaining to this project maintained in the District Collector's office, along with a brief one-line description of each file.

5. Copy of the **land acquisition proposal** submitted by PWD or [EXECUTING AGENCY e.g. KRFB] to the Revenue Department for initiating acquisition proceedings.

6. If any meetings were conducted under the District Collector regarding this project in the year **[YEAR1] and [YEAR2]**, please provide copies of the **minutes and proceedings** of such meetings.

I request that the information be provided in **digital format** (email/CD/pen drive) to avoid delay and unnecessary cost.

## Why these questions work

| Question | What it reveals |
|---|---|
| Section 11(1) notification | Whether acquisition has even started |
| Section 19(1) declaration | Whether land is officially earmarked |
| Government Order / sanction | Whether the project has funding approval |
| File list | All files related to project — useful for follow-up RTIs |
| Land acquisition proposal | Who submitted it and when — reveals delays between agencies |
| Meeting minutes | Internal deliberations, bottlenecks, decisions |
