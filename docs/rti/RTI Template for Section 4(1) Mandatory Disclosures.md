# RTI Template: Section 4(1) Mandatory Disclosures

Most public offices are required to proactively publish information under Section 4(1)(b) of the RTI Act, 2005 — but the majority don't. Filing an RTI asking about this puts them on record and often prompts them to publish what they should have already made public.

**How to use this template:** Replace `[OFFICE NAME]`, `[DISTRICT]`, and `[OFFICIAL WEBSITE URL]` with the relevant office details. File with the Public Information Officer of that office.

---

## RTI Application Template

Sir/Madam,

Under the Right to Information Act, 2005, I hereby request the following information:

1. Whether the **[OFFICE NAME], [DISTRICT]** has published the mandatory disclosures under **Section 4(1)(b) of the RTI Act, 2005** on the official website at **[OFFICIAL WEBSITE URL]**.

2. If **yes**, please provide:
   - a. The direct URL/web link where the Section 4 disclosures for [DISTRICT] can be accessed on the said website.
   - b. Certified copy of the latest published disclosures under Section 4(1)(b) in electronic format.

3. If **no**, please provide the following:
   - a. Reasons for non-publication of Section 4 disclosures on the official website.
   - b. Current status of preparation and publication of these mandatory disclosures.
   - c. Expected date of publication on the official website.
   - d. Copy of all mandatory disclosures under Section 4(1)(b) clauses (i) to (xvii) for [DISTRICT] in electronic format.

4. Specifically regarding **Section 4(1)(b)(vi)**, please provide a complete statement of categories of documents that are held by [OFFICE NAME], [DISTRICT] or under its control.

5. Copies of any correspondence or directions from the State/Central Information Commission or Department regarding the publication of Section 4 disclosures for [DISTRICT].

6. Name and designation of the official responsible for publishing and maintaining the Section 4 disclosures for [OFFICE NAME], [DISTRICT] on the official website.

7. Bank account details including account number, IFSC code, account holder name, and branch name where the application fee and additional charges for photocopies or documents can be remitted online.

I request that the information be provided via email. Please provide point-wise responses to each of the above questions separately.

## Why this works

Section 4(1)(b) lists 17 categories of information every public authority must proactively publish. Most don't. This RTI:

| Question | Effect |
|---|---|
| Q1–3: Publication status | Forces a yes/no answer on compliance — either way, it's on record |
| Q3d: Copies of all disclosures | Gets you the information even if it was never published online |
| Q4: Document categories (vi) | Reveals what files the office holds — useful for follow-up RTIs |
| Q5: SIC/commission directions | Uncovers if the office has already been told to comply and ignored it |
| Q6: Responsible official | Identifies who is accountable — useful for complaints |
| Q7: Bank details | Removes the excuse of "we don't know how to accept fees online" |

## The 17 categories under Section 4(1)(b)

(i) Organisation structure · (ii) Powers and duties of officers · (iii) Decision-making process · (iv) Norms for discharge of functions · (v) Rules and regulations · **(vi) Categories of documents held** · (vii) Consultative arrangements with public · (viii) Boards/councils/committees · (ix) Directory of officers · (x) Monthly remuneration · (xi) Budget allocation · (xii) Subsidy programmes · (xiii) Concessions and permits · (xiv) Electronic data · (xv) Information available in electronic form · (xvi) Facilities for obtaining information · (xvii) Names and designations of PIOs
