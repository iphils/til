# RTI Template: Follow Up on an Unanswered Grievance Petition

If you filed a formal grievance (e.g., CM With Me, CPGRAMS, or any state/central portal) and received no meaningful action, filing an RTI asking for the complete file on your complaint often triggers officers into action — they know their internal notes and inaction will now become visible.

**How to use this template:** Replace `[PORTAL NAME]`, `[COMPLAINT ID 1]`, `[COMPLAINT ID 2]`, and `[GOVERNMENT/DEPARTMENT NAME]` with your details. File with the PIO of the department your complaint was forwarded to, or the nodal department managing the grievance portal.

---

## RTI Application Template

**Subject:** Request for information under Right to Information Act, 2005 — Grievance complaints [COMPLAINT ID 1] and [COMPLAINT ID 2]

Respected Sir/Madam,

I am writing to request information under the Right to Information Act, 2005 regarding complaints filed through the **[PORTAL NAME]** grievance redressal scheme of the **[GOVERNMENT/DEPARTMENT NAME]**.

**INFORMATION REQUESTED:**

I have filed complaints through the **[PORTAL NAME]** portal with the following reference numbers:

1. [COMPLAINT ID 1]
2. [COMPLAINT ID 2]

Under Section 4 and Section 6 of the RTI Act, 2005, I request the following information:

1. Complete copies of all files, documents, and records pertaining to complaint ID **[COMPLAINT ID 1]**, including:
   - a) Original complaint submission details
   - b) All internal noting and correspondence
   - c) Action taken reports and status updates
   - d) Recommendations and decisions made
   - e) Final disposal order/resolution, if any

2. Complete copies of all files, documents, and records pertaining to complaint ID **[COMPLAINT ID 2]**, including:
   - a) Original complaint submission details
   - b) All internal noting and correspondence
   - c) Action taken reports and status updates
   - d) Recommendations and decisions made
   - e) Final disposal order/resolution, if any

3. Details of the current status of both complaints and the officials/departments to whom these complaints were forwarded for action.

4. Any other relevant documents or records maintained in connection with these complaints.

**MODE OF DELIVERY:** I request that the information be provided in electronic format via email.

## Why this works

Grievance portals often close complaints with a generic "resolved" status without taking any real action. Asking for the complete file under RTI:

| What you ask for | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Internal noting and correspondence | Reveals what officers actually said/did internally |
| Action taken reports | Exposes whether "resolved" was genuine or fabricated |
| Forwarding details | Shows if the complaint was passed around without action |
| Final disposal order | Forces the office to produce or admit there is no resolution |

Requesting **all internal noting** is particularly powerful — officers are reluctant to write dismissive notes when they know those notes can be RTI-ed.

## Tips

- **File with the right PIO:** If the portal forwarded your complaint to a specific department, file the RTI with that department's PIO, not just the portal's nodal officer.
- **One complaint per RTI application** is cleaner if the complaints went to different departments.
- **If still no action after RTI reply:** file a First Appeal, and then escalate to the State/Central Information Commission.
